MEET MICHAEL

Evidence-Based Leadership. Community Commitment.

Michael Braun is a professor of criminal justice and longtime educator who brings an evidence-based, practical approach to public service. He believes effective local government requires careful stewardship of public resources, transparent decision-making, and a focus on the long-term needs of the community.

As a candidate for the Walnut Valley Water District Board of Directors, Michael is focused on maintaining a reliable water supply, keeping rates responsible, investing wisely in infrastructure, and ensuring that decisions are made openly and with the interests of ratepayers in mind.

PRIORITIES

Reliable Water. Responsible Management.

01

Affordable, Responsible Rates

Water rates should reflect the real cost of providing reliable service while maintaining discipline over spending and keeping the district accountable to ratepayers.

02

Reliable Infrastructure

Maintaining hundreds of miles of water mains, reservoirs, pumps, and other infrastructure requires clear priorities and careful oversight of capital spending.

03

Long-Term Water Security

Our region depends heavily on imported water. The district should plan now for where our water will come from 10, 20, and more years into the future.

04

Transparency & Accountability

Ratepayers should be able to understand how rates are determined, where their money is going, which projects are being prioritized, and why.

05

Emergency & Drought Resilience

The water system must remain dependable during droughts, wildfires, power outages, and infrastructure failures while continuing responsible conservation and demand management.

Reliable water requires planning for the long term, discipline with ratepayer dollars, and accountability for every decision.
